Pack by planned sessions, not by number of days

A three-day fitness staycation can include very different activities, so count sessions rather than simply packing three outfits. Write down the actual plan—gym, pool, walking, yoga, brunch or recovery—and note which pieces can realistically be reworn after they are fully dry. This prevents both overpacking and the opposite problem of relying on one garment that becomes uncomfortable after a sweaty session.

Separate the items that are hard to substitute, such as a trusted sports bra or training shoe, from flexible pieces such as casual layers. Add a simple laundry or drying plan if the hotel stay includes repeated workouts. A small mesh bag for used activewear can also keep damp clothing away from clean pieces until it can dry or be washed. The goal is a usable rotation, not a suitcase full of backup outfits.
