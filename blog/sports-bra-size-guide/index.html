Sports Bra Size Guide: How to Check Band, Cups and Straps

Use measurements and movement tests to evaluate sports-bra band fit, cup coverage, fixed pads and criss-cross straps.

Sports-bra sizing differs between brands, so the size worn in everyday bras or another activewear label is only a starting point. Use the current product chart and your latest measurements.

A good fit remains stable during movement, supports the intended impact level and allows normal breathing.

Quick checklist
  • Use the current brand size chart.
  • Take fresh measurements.
  • Confirm the band stays level.
  • Check cup coverage from front and sides.

Measure consistently

Wear a non-padded bra or follow the brand’s measurement instructions. Keep the tape level and comfortably close without pulling it tight.

Measurements change over time, so do not rely on an old number when fit has changed.

Evaluate the band

The band should sit level around the body. It should not ride up when the arms lift or slide during movement.

You should be able to breathe deeply. Painful pressure is not a requirement for support.

Check cup coverage

Look for spillage at the top or sides and large empty areas. The center and underarm coverage should remain secure when bending and reaching.

Fixed pads should align naturally and remain smooth.

Test straps and back

Criss-cross straps should stay in place without digging. Move the shoulders through circles, overhead reaches and pulling motions.

If the straps carry nearly all the pressure, the band fit may need attention.

Recheck after washing

Follow the care label and use a mesh bag when appropriate. Heat and twisting can affect the band and pads.

Replace the bra when elastic recovery and support noticeably decline.

Practical buying and care checklist

  • Use the current brand size chart.
  • Take fresh measurements.
  • Confirm the band stays level.
  • Check cup coverage from front and sides.
  • Test straps through full arm movement.
  • Reassess support after repeated washing.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my normal bra size?

Use it as a starting point only; compare with the product’s current chart.

Should the band leave marks?

Light temporary marks can occur, but painful, deep or persistent marks suggest excessive pressure.

Why do fixed pads wrinkle?

Fit, washing stress or distortion can affect them. Use gentle care and reshape as allowed.

What if I fall between sizes?

Follow the brand’s guidance and consider the activity, desired compression and return options.
