How to Wash Activewear Without Damaging Stretch and Shape

KAVOMAZ EditorialPublished 2026-06-23

Follow a simple activewear washing routine for leggings, sports bras and seamless tops while protecting stretch, pads and color.

Activewear traps sweat and body oils differently from heavy cotton clothing. A good wash routine removes residue without exposing stretch fibers, pads and seams to unnecessary heat or friction.

The care label is the final authority. The steps below are a general routine that should be adjusted when the manufacturer gives different instructions.

Quick checklist
  • Empty pockets and close hooks.
  • Turn garments inside out.
  • Use a gentle cycle and measured detergent.
  • Skip fabric softener unless permitted.

Before the wash

Empty every pocket, close hooks and turn garments inside out. Separate activewear from towels, denim and items with sharp zippers.

Let extremely damp pieces air briefly if they cannot be washed immediately, but do not leave them in a closed gym bag.

Choose the cycle and detergent

Use cool water and a gentle cycle when allowed by the label. Measure mild liquid detergent instead of adding extra for odor.

Skip automatic fabric softener because residue can coat technical fabrics. Avoid chlorine bleach unless the label explicitly permits it.

Protect sports bras and pads

Place sports bras in a mesh bag to reduce twisting. Fixed pads should remain smooth; reshape them gently after washing if necessary.

Do not wring the bra because twisting can distort the band and cups.

Dry with low stress

Air-drying is often the safest option for stretch garments when the label permits. Lay items flat or hang them in a way that does not stretch the shoulders.

Avoid high dryer heat and strong direct sunlight for long periods.

Handle persistent odor

First check for detergent buildup, an overloaded machine or slow drying. Rewash with the correct detergent amount and good rinsing rather than masking odor with more fragrance.

Clean the washing machine regularly because residue inside the drum can transfer back to clothing.

Practical buying and care checklist

  • Empty pockets and close hooks.
  • Turn garments inside out.
  • Use a gentle cycle and measured detergent.
  • Skip fabric softener unless permitted.
  • Use a mesh bag for sports bras.
  • Air-dry away from excessive heat.

Frequently asked questions

Can vinegar be used on activewear?

Care advice varies by fabric and manufacturer. Use only when the product instructions permit it and never mix it with bleach.

Why should activewear be washed inside out?

It reduces surface friction and exposes the sweat-contact side more directly to the wash.

Can leggings go in the dryer?

Only when the care label allows it; lower heat is generally safer for stretch.

How do I stop sports-bra pads from folding?

Use a mesh bag, avoid aggressive spinning when possible and reshape fixed pads gently after washing.
